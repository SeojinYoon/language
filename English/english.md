# 영어 구조 정리

- **원인과 결과(Causality)**
    - 일반
        - ~했다 ~이어서
            - $\main{S + V}$ **because*- $\sub{S + V}$
                - 정보 전달 중심
                - 예문
                    - $\main{I sang as much as I wanted}$ because $\sub{nobody paid attention to me singing}$
                    - $\main{I wasn't very excited}$ because $\sub{they always end up dried an withered within three days}$.
        
            - $\main{S + V so Adj}$ **that*- $\sub{S + V}$
                - 심각성 호소, 하소연
                - 예문
                    - $\main{I am so embarrassed}$ that $\sub{the black men forced me to pay}$ for the bracelet they put on me
            
    - ~해서 ~했다
        - $\sub{S + V}$ **so*- $\main{S + V}$
            - $\sub{I forgot to change money beforehand}$ so $\main{I did it at the airport}$
        - $\sub{S + V}$ and **end up*- $\main{Prep. Phrase / Adj. / V-ing}$
            - $\sub{I once misplaced my son for a minute}$ and $\main{ended up in panic}$
        - **Since*- $\sub{S + V}$, $\main{S + V}$
            - Since $\sub{there are too many events in a year}$, $\main{I always mark them on a calendar}$
        - $\sub{S + V so Adj}$ **that*- $\main{S + V}$
            - $\sub{My parents were so strict}$ that $\main{I had to come back home before midnight}$. 
        - $\sub{so (원인)}$ **as to*- $\main{be p.p}$: 너무 ~해서 결국 ~하게 되다
            - Actually, the margin of my energy over tasks is so small as to be exhausted

- **부정**
    - ~인 것은 ~때문이 아니다
        - not $\main{A}$ because $\sub{B}$
            - You are not happy because you are rich
        - neither $\main{A}$ nor $\main{A}$ because $\sub{B}$
            - You are neither right nor wrong because the crowd disagrees with you
    - 결코 ~이 아니다
        - by no means
            - Fortunately for the typical investor, It is by no means necessary for his success that he bring these qualities to bear upon his program

- **기대와 반전**
    - $\sub{S + V}$ **but*- $\main{S + V}$
        - 어떤 상황이었는데 ~ 이렇게 되다라고 표현할 때 유용한듯
            - $\sub{I studied hard}$ **but*- $\main{I failed the exam}$

    - [한국어 사고] ~하지만 ~하다 <-> [영어 사고] ~하지만 ~하다
        - $\sub{S + V}$ **but*- $\main{S + V}$
            - $\sub{I am very shy}$ **but*- $\main{I will try to talk to her first}$

- **정보의 기원과 출처**
    - [한국어 사고] ~으로/한테 ~하다 <-> [영어 사고] ~하다 ~으로/한테
        - $\main{S + V}$ **from*- $\sub{N}$
            - $\main{I got this gift}$ from $\sub{my girlfriend}$
    - [한국어 사고] ~때문에 ~으로/~한테 ~하다 <-> [영어 사고] ~하다 ~으로/~한테 ~때문에
        - $\main{S + V}$ **from*- $\sub{N as S + V}$
            - $\main{I got this present}$ from $\sub{her as she was celebrating our anniversary}$
        - $\main{S + V}$ **from*- $\sub{N, V-ing}$
            - I got this present from her, celebrating our anniversary
- **상태의 변화**
    - get p.p
        - I got excited about the new research topic
    - get 기분 (상태)
        - I get happy whenever I read books
    - become
        - 결과적으로 ~가 되다, 혹은 ~로 성장하다는 깊이 있는 느낌을 줌
            - The agent became more efficient after training

- **목적**
    - [한국어 사고] ~하게 ~하다 <-> [영어 사고] ~하다 ~하게
        - $\main{S + V}$ **so that*- $\sub{S + V}$
            - 미래의 가능성이나 결과를 위해
            - (긍정형) $\main{I study reinforcement learning everyday}$ so that $\sub{I can become a researcher at DeepMind}$
            - $\main{we need to take turns}$ so that $\sub{everyone can sing}$
            - (부정형) $\main{I marked it on the calendar}$ so that $\sub{I can't forget it}$
        - $\main{S + V}$ **to*- $\sub{V}$
            - 가장 가볍고 흔하게 쓰임
            - $\main{I study English}$ to $\sub{survive in Geneva}$
        - $\main{S + V}$ **in order to*- $\sub{V}$
            - 오직 이 목적을 위해서라고 강조할 때 쓰임
            - We updated the model in order to increase accuracy
        - $\main{S + V}$ **for*- $\sub{N}$
            - 동사가 아닌 사람이나, 사물, 이유를 위해 무언가를 할 때 쓰임
            - I bought some chocolate for Oscar
        - $\main{S + V}$ **such that*- $\sub{S + V}$
            - 어떤 조치를 취해서 그 결과가 특정한 상태가 되도록 만들 때 씀
                - The system was designed such that anyone could use it

- **시간**
    - [한국어 사고] ~하면 ~하다 <-> [영어 사고] ~하면 ~하다
        - When $\sub{S + V}$, $\main{S + V}$ 
            - $\sub{When you are out on Christmas day}$, $\main{all you can see are lovely couples}$.

    - **시간의 경과**
        - [한국어 사고] ~가 지나니 ~하다 <-> [영어 사고] ~가 지나니 ~하다
            - After $\sub{N}$, $\main{S + V}$
                - After $\sub{3 years}$, $\main{my creative gift ideas are running a little low}$.

    - **시간의 경과 (Sequence)**- 
        - **After*- $\sub{N}$, $\main{S + V}$
            - After intense diet, I experienced hair loss
        - $\sub{S + have p.p}$ **and*- $\main{S + V}$
            - $\sub{I've taken diet pills}$ and $\main{I lost a lot of weight}$
        - $\sub{S + V}$ **and*- $\main{S + V}$
            - I did intense diet and I experienced hair loss
    - **지속**
        - ever since: 그 시점부터 지금까지 줄곧, 단 한번도 빠짐없이
            - Ever since he has been with Don Quixote he has been dreaming of an "island" of which he would be governor

- **동시**
    - [한국어 사고] ~하는 동안에 ~하다 <-> [영어 사고] ~하다 ~하는 동안에
        - $\main{S + V}$ **while*- $\sub{S + V}$
            - $\main{Someone stole my laptop}$ while $\sub{I was in the restroom}$
        - When I was young, I fell over while riding a bike

- **경험**
    - [한국어 사고] $\sub{한번은 ~했다가}$, $\main{~했다}$ <-> [영어 사고] $\sub{~했다가 한번은}$, $\main{~했다}$
        - S + V once, S + V
            - $\sub{I went to a singing room}$ once, and we were so hungry that $\main{we came out within 30 minutes}$
    - being
        - 그 당시의 존재 방식 자체가 경험의 중심이 됨
            - Being alone in Geneva for the first time was challenging but remarkable
    - ever since
        - 단순히 since가 시점을 찍는 것이라면, ever since는 그 시점부터 지금까지의 모든 시간을 하나의 선으로 잇는 힘이 있다

- **빈도**
    - [한국어 사고] ~할때 마다 ~한다 <-> [영어 사고] ~할때 마다 ~한다
        - **whenever*- $\sub{S + V}$, $\main{S + V}$
            - Whenever $\sub{I go}$, $\main{they give me free beverages}$

- **수단**
    - [한국어 사고] ~해서 ~하다 <-> [영어 사고] ~해서 ~하다 (**Method**)
        - $\main{S + V}$ **by*- $\sub{V-ing}$
            - $\main{I usually remember directions}$ $\sub{by looking at buildings around me}$.
            - $\main{I aligned the data}$ $\sub{by using DTW}$, $\subb{so that I could compare trials with different speeds}$
    - [한국어 사고] ~으로 ~하다 <-> [영어 사고] ~하다 ~으로 (**Tool**)
        - $\main{S + V}$ **with*- $\sub{N}$
            - $\main{I could avoid heavy traffic}$ with $\sub{GPS application}$
    - [한국어 사고] ~으로 ~하다 <-> [영어 사고] ~하다 ~으로 (**특정 Tool이나 Method를 목적을 위해 '능동적'으로 사용했는걸 강조**)
        - $\main{S + V}$ **using*- $\sub{N}$
            - $\main{I estimated 3D positions}$ using $\sub{pose estimation}$
    - by being 형용사: ~함으로써
        - I always being bigger person by being extra patient and understanding people who think differently from me

- **대조(Contrast)**
    - [한국어 사고] ~했지만 한다 <-> [영어 사고] ~했지만 ~한다
        - **Although*- $\sub{S + V}$, $\main{S + V}$
            - 문장 맨 앞에 위치하여, 뒤에 올 반전을 미리 예고함
            - Although $\sub{I was exhaused}$, $\main{I finished the study}$
        - **Even though*- $\sub{S + V}$, $\main{S + V}$
            - Although보다 더 강조하는 느낌
    - [한국어 사고] ~한다 ~하지만 <-> [영어 사고] ~한다 ~하지만
        - $\main{S + V}$, **but*- $\sub{S + V}$
            - 앞선 사실을 뒤집기
            - I was tired, but I didn't skip lunch
        - $\main{S + V}$ **yet*- $\sub{S + V}$
            - but 보다 더 격식 있고 그럼에도 불구하고라는 뉘앙스가 강함
        - **Despite*- + $\sub{N}$, $\main{S + V}$
            - Despite the exhaustion, I finished the study
        - **In spite of*- + $\sub{N}$, $\main{S + V}$

- **양보(Concession)**
    - $\main{S + V}$, **However**, $\sub{S + V}$
    - $\main{S + V}$, **Nevertheless**, $\sub{S + V}$
        - I was tired. Nevertheless, I didn't skip lunch
    - even though
        - 이미 일어났거나 현재 벌어지고 있는 실제 사실을 인정할 때 씀. 비록 (실제로) ~이지만, 그럼에도 불구하고라는 뜻
            - Even though I spent three weeks on the conversion, there are still some bugs
    - even if
        - 아직 일어나지 않았거나 가능성이 낮은 가상 상황을 설정할 때 씀. 만약 (설령) ~하더라도, 결과는 변함없을 거야라는 뜻
